BIOSTASI is an early-stage international initiative exploring cryobiology, biological preservation and future regenerative technologies. We are connecting people who may help turn a long-term vision into a responsible, evidence-led scientific program.

We welcome expressions of interest. No research positions, compensation, equity arrangements or institutional partnerships are currently being offered or guaranteed.

Scientific collaborators

Cryobiology, organ and brain preservation, perfusion, biomedical research and related disciplines.

Potential co-founders

People interested in helping develop the initiative's scientific, organizational and long-term strategy.

Engineering & technology

Cryogenic engineering, instrumentation, software, data systems and technical infrastructure.

Community & supporters

Scientific communication, international networking, responsible advocacy and other relevant contributions.
