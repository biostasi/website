Research Focus

Scientific areas of interest

BIOSTASI studies scientific developments that may contribute to long-term biological preservation and future recovery.

🧬 Cryobiology

Research on the response of cells and tissues to low temperatures and cryoprotective methods.

🧠 Brain Preservation

Exploring approaches that aim to preserve neural structure and biological information.

❤️ Organ Preservation

Monitoring advances in perfusion, vitrification and long-term organ storage.

🔬 Regenerative Technologies

Following developments in biotechnology, tissue engineering and molecular repair.
