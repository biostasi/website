Mission & Vision

Preserving possibility through science.

BIOSTASI exists to examine the scientific pathways that may one day support long-term preservation and future recovery, while promoting rigorous research, ethical responsibility and international collaboration.

Our Mission

To advance understanding of biostasis, cryobiology and preservation technologies through credible research, transparent documentation and responsible scientific dialogue.

Our Vision

A future in which preservation science protects biological information and expands the possibilities available to medicine.

Research Focus

Scientific areas shaping the future of preservation.

The initiative follows developments across interconnected fields that may contribute to preserving biological structure, function and information.

01

Cryobiology

Cellular and tissue responses to low temperatures, cooling methods and cryoprotective strategies.

02

Brain Preservation

Approaches intended to preserve neural structure, connectivity and biologically encoded information.

03

Organ Preservation

Advances in perfusion, vitrification and extended organ storage for future clinical applications.

04

Regenerative Technologies

Tissue engineering, molecular repair and computational systems that may support future recovery.
