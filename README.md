# BIOSTASI
*Preserve Today. Protect Tomorrow.*
## Life. Paused. Not Lost.

BIOSTASI is an international research initiative exploring the future of biological preservation.

Our long-term vision is to investigate scientific pathways that may one day allow temporary biostasis, enabling future medicine to restore health that is currently beyond today's medical capabilities.

---

## Research Areas

- Biostasis
- Cryomedicine
- Regenerative Medicine
- Molecular Engineering
- Biotechnology
- Future Medical Technologies

---

## Mission

To advance research into long-term biological preservation while promoting science, ethics and responsible innovation.

---

## Vision

BIOSTASI aims to support research into future technologies that could one day make long-term biological preservation a practical medical option for humans and animals.

Our objective is to build an international scientific ecosystem where cryomedicine, regenerative medicine, biotechnology, molecular engineering, robotics and artificial intelligence converge toward a common goal:

**Extending the possibilities of future medicine.**
